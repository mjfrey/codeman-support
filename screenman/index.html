// FEATURES

Your Mac, in Your Hand

Built for speed and detail, on your network or away from home.

Fast and Sharp

Your Mac streams its screen as HEVC video, in full 4:4:4 color on devices that decode it in hardware, so small text stays crisp and the pointer keeps up with your finger.

Zoom Into Detail

Pinch to zoom and your Mac sends just the part you’re looking at, at full resolution. Two fingers pan the view; the view can follow the pointer.

Touch or Trackpad

Touch the Mac’s screen directly—tap to click, swipe to scroll, hold and move to drag—or turn your screen into a trackpad with a pointer, like on a MacBook.

A Laptop in Your Hand

On iPhone, the laptop layout puts a full Mac keyboard—with ⌘, ⌥, ⌃ and arrow keys—and a trackpad under the picture. Save your Mac’s password and send it with Face ID.

Hear Your Mac

Your Mac’s sound plays on your iPhone or iPad, with a buffer that rides out Wi‑Fi hiccups instead of skipping.

Home, Away, on Cellular

Full detail on your home network. Over a VPN or 5G, ScreenMan adjusts quality and resolution to the connection so it stays responsive.

Asleep or Signed Out

ScreenMan can wake a sleeping Mac with Wake on LAN where your Mac supports it, and works at the login window, so you can sign in from your iPhone.

Private by Design

Pair once with a QR code; after that your devices recognize each other by their keys. Everything is encrypted end to end. No account, no server, no tracking.

// SETUP

Set Up in Three Steps

About two minutes. The apps walk you through each step.

1

Install ScreenMan Host on your Mac

Download it and open the installer. It runs quietly in the menu bar—look for a screen with a pointer.

2

Allow it on your Mac

A setup window shows the two permissions ScreenMan Host needs—screen & audio recording, and accessibility—with a button straight to each one.

3

Pair your iPhone or iPad

Click Pair iPhone or iPad… on the Mac, then tap Scan QR Code in ScreenMan. That’s it.

Quick answers to common questions about ScreenMan.

ScreenMan shows your Mac’s screen on your iPhone or iPad and lets you control it with touch, a trackpad layout, and a full Mac keyboard, with your Mac’s sound. It has two parts: the ScreenMan app on your iPhone or iPad, and ScreenMan Host, a small Mac app that shares the screen.

Yes, through a VPN that connects your iPhone or iPad to your home network (for example Tailscale or WireGuard). ScreenMan tries every address your Mac reports and adjusts quality to the connection, so it stays usable on cellular too.
Screen & System Audio Recording lets it show your screen and play its sound on your iPhone or iPad. Accessibility lets your taps, clicks, and typing control the Mac. On macOS 27 both are a single switch under Privacy & Security → Device Control and Data Access. If the switch is on but the setup window still says “Not allowed yet”, remove ScreenMan Host from the list with − and add it again.
Yes. Your iPhone or iPad connects directly to your Mac over an encrypted connection; nothing passes through our servers, because there are none. Only devices you’ve paired can connect, and you can unpair any of them from the Mac’s menu bar. We collect no data—see the privacy policy.
