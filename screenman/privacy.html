Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

ScreenMan does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information. Your screen, sound, and typing go directly between your own devices, encrypted, and never through us.

Overview

ScreenMan has two parts: the ScreenMan app on your iPhone or iPad, and ScreenMan Host on your Mac. They connect directly to each other. We run no servers, have no accounts, and include no analytics.

Data We Collect

None. Specifically:

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • No usage data or analytics
  • No crash reports or diagnostics
  • No device identifiers or fingerprinting
  • No location data
  • No cookies or tracking technologies

Your Screen, Sound, and Input

Your Mac’s picture and sound, and your taps, clicks, and typing, travel only between your Mac and the iPhone or iPad you paired with it, over an encrypted connection (TLS 1.3). Text you copy on the Mac can be pasted on your iPhone or iPad, and text you paste can be sent to the Mac, only between those devices.

Update Checks

ScreenMan Host on your Mac checks this website about once a day for a newer version, and when you choose Check for Updates. That is an ordinary download of a small file describing the latest version: nothing about you, your Mac or your devices is sent, and we keep no record of it. You can turn the daily check off in the ScreenMan menu bar menu (Check for Updates Automatically). Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log requests as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

What Stays on Your Devices

  • Pairing: each device’s key and the list of devices it’s paired with, so they can recognize each other. On iPhone and iPad these are kept in the Keychain.
  • Your Mac’s password, only if you choose to save it: kept in the Keychain on your iPhone or iPad, unlocked with Face ID, and sent only to that Mac when you tap Enter Password.
  • A picture of each Mac’s last screen, shown on the home screen.
  • Logs on the Mac, for troubleshooting. They never leave your Mac unless you send them to us.

Permissions

  • Camera (iPhone, iPad): only to scan the pairing QR code your Mac shows. Images aren’t stored or sent anywhere.
  • Local Network (iPhone, iPad): only to reach your own Mac, and to wake it with Wake on LAN.
  • Face ID (iPhone, iPad): only to unlock a Mac password you chose to save.
  • Screen & System Audio Recording, Accessibility (Mac): to show the screen and play its sound on your paired devices, and to let them control the Mac.

Third-Party Services

ScreenMan contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services, and no ads.

Support Contact Form

If you choose to contact us through the support form on this website, the information you voluntarily provide (name, email, message) is processed by Formspree and delivered to us via email. It is used solely to respond to your inquiry and is not shared with anyone else.

Children’s Privacy

Since we do not collect any data, there are no special concerns regarding children’s privacy.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this privacy policy, we will update this page with the new effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the contact form.
